Grandmaster History

The Chess Legends

Magnus Carlsen

Click to Reveal ▾

The "Mozart of Chess" and one of the most dominant World Champions in history.

  • Playing Style: Universal style with peerless endgame technique.
  • Peak Rating: 2882 (Highest in chess history).
  • Legacy: Multiple-time World Classical, Rapid, and Blitz Champion.

Garry Kasparov

Click to Reveal ▾

A fierce tactical genius who held the world number one spot for 20 consecutive years.

  • Playing Style: Hyper-aggressive, highly prepared opening innovator.
  • Peak Rating: 2851.
  • Legacy: Famous for his historic matches against IBM's Deep Blue supercomputer.

Bobby Fischer

Click to Reveal ▾

The American prodigy who single-handedly broke the Soviet chess monopoly.

  • Playing Style: Relentless precision and deep psychological dominance.
  • Peak Rating: 2785.
  • Legacy: Won the legendary 1972 World Championship Match against Boris Spassky.

Mikhail Tal

Click to Reveal ▾

Known as the "Magician from Riga," famous for mind-bending sacrifices.

  • Playing Style: Wild, chaotic, and ultra-attacking sacrifices.
  • Peak Rating: World Champion (8th).
  • Legacy: Proved that psychological pressure and complications can break any defense.
